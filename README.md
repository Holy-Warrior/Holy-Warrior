### 👋 About Me

I'm a Software Engineering student who ended up comfortable across more stacks than I planned on — Flutter for mobile, Laravel/PHP and JS for the web, and lately Rust + Tauri for desktop. I pick the tool the project actually needs rather than sticking to one lane, and I fold in Generative AI when it genuinely improves what I'm building, not just for the sake of it.

- 🎓 BS in Software Engineering @ Sarhad University of Science and Information Technology, Peshawar
- 🧠 Final Year Project: **DeenLab** — an Islamic utility app with an on-device ML engine (Human Activity Recognition) that auto-silences your phone during prayer
- 🛠️ Currently building **Comic Tracker**, my first Rust/Tauri v2 desktop app
- 🌱 Learning: systems-level programming, on-device ML deployment
- 📫 Reach me: **cutexcoder@gmail.com** · [LinkedIn](https://linkedin.com/in/hamza-bin-zafar-cutexcoder)
